# Intima Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 August 2026

Intima is a Garmin Connect IQ application that provides manually started intimacy wellness tracking for consenting adults.

## Data handled by the app

During a session, Intima temporarily processes heart rate and accelerometer data on the Garmin device. It uses that data to estimate active time, movement rhythm, rhythm consistency, relative motion intensity, and short-term heart-rate recovery. It also displays Garmin's activity calorie estimate. Users may add manual highlight markers. Motion metrics are experimental estimates and do not infer arousal, climax, consent, sexual position, partner satisfaction, or whether sexual activity occurred.

An activity remains unsaved until the user explicitly chooses Save from the summary. If the user chooses Discard or exits before confirming, Intima discards the pending FIT activity. A saved FIT activity may include elapsed time, calories, heart rate, manual lap/highlight markers, Intima summary fields, and other activity data recorded by the Garmin platform. Intima stores the durations of the last eight explicitly saved sessions in local application storage to draw Personal Trend; discarded sessions are not included.

## Data collected by the developer

The developer of Intima does not collect, receive, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data through the app. Intima has no developer-operated account, server, analytics service, advertising service, or third-party tracking SDK. The app does not request or record GPS location.

## Storage and control

FIT activities are stored and managed by the Garmin device and Garmin services according to the user's Garmin settings and Garmin's privacy practices. The eight-value duration trend remains in Intima's local application storage and is overwritten as newer sessions are saved. A saved activity may be visible to other people based on the user's Garmin Connect privacy settings. Intima gives saved activities the discreet name “Private Cardio,” but users should review their Garmin Connect privacy settings before recording. Users can discard a recording before it is saved, or view, edit, and delete saved activities using their Garmin device or Garmin Connect. Data submitted to or stored by Garmin is governed by Garmin's policies and is not submitted to the developer.

## Children

Intima is intended only for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

## Changes

If Intima's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The effective date above will also be updated.
